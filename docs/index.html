§16.1 Repetition is a property of the route’s shape, not of its stops — an out-and-back on one corridor will always repeat. 重复是路线形状的属性，不是站点的属性——原路折返必然重复。 §17.1 A number you did not read this session is a number you invented — and it happens in the cell you fill to finish a row, not in the fact you set out to research. 你这轮没读过的数字就是编的——而且它发生在「为了补齐表格而填的那一格」。

Data boundaries ｜ 数据边界

  • Everything expires. Prices, opening hours, schedules and room inventory change. Collection dates are recorded throughout — re-verify against the operator’s own page before you travel. 一切都会过期。文中标注了采集日期，出行前必须以运营方自己的页面为准。
  • Ratings are labelled by platform and never mixed. A number without a platform label is a bug. 评分一律标注平台、绝不混用；没标平台的数字是错误。
  • “Not found” means the search actually failed — never a placeholder for a guess. 「未查到」表示确实检索未果，不是猜测的占位符。
  • Route maps are for planning; they do not replace live navigation, road-closure feeds or weather warnings. 路线图用于规划，不替代实时导航、道路封闭与天气警报。
